Fastest route to top-tier pay from where you are

  1. Finish ACA. "ACA qualified" unlocks most roles flagged below and adds roughly £15–25k to your market rate.
  2. Ask about an internal move into your firm's Deal Advisory / Transaction Services team. Audit to TS is the standard path, and internal moves skip the degree screen.
  3. After 1–2 years in TS: PE portfolio finance, corporate development, or lateral IB associate roles (often £90k–£150k+ with bonus).
  4. Apply now to the realistic-reach roles here that already accept part-qualified ACA, and treat the rest as targets.
